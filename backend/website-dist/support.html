How do I delete my account?

In the Path+ app, go to Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, moments, messages, and friend connections. You can also email dev@pathplus.store from your registered address if you cannot access the app.

How do I report content or a user?

Use the report controls on a moment, comment, or profile inside the app. You can also block a user to stop further interaction. We review reports and take action, including removing content or suspending accounts when needed.

Privacy questions?

Read our Privacy Policy, or email privacy@pathplus.store for data access or deletion requests.

Response time

We aim to respond to support emails within 1–2 business days. Safety reports submitted in-app are reviewed as a priority.
